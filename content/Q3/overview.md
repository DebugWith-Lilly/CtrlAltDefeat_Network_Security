# Five-Zone Security Architecture

1. **Corporate Zone** — Employee devices and everyday business systems such as ERP and email. 
2. **OT Zone** — PLCs, SCADA, sensors and manufacturing control systems; isolated to protect production. 
3. **Guest Zone** — Visitor devices with **Internet-only access** and no access to internal resources. 
4. **Servers/DMZ Zone** — Public-facing services such as the e-commerce platform, separated from the internal network. 
5. **Management Zone** — Network administration systems; restricted to authorised administrators using **RBAC and MFA**.
